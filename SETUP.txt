THE SLAYGENCY - ENQUIRY FORM SETUP

1. Open index.html using Live Server or a hosted website.
2. Submit a test enquiry with your own details.
3. Check enquiry.theslaygency@gmail.com for the FormSubmit activation email. Check spam too.
4. Follow the activation link, then submit a second test enquiry and confirm receipt.
5. This uses FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service; update your website privacy notice before launch.
6. FormSubmit may enforce spam checks or additional verification. Test after publishing.
7. No DNS settings are needed for a regular @gmail.com address.
